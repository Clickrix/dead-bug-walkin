Know the enemy

Bed bugs don’t stand a chance — if you act early

Bed bugs sneak in on furniture, clothing and luggage, then multiply fast. Most people don’t realize they still have them for 2–6 months after a failed DIY treatment.

Store-bought sprays often don’t kill bed bugs once dry — and can spread the colony deeper into your home. Our specialists stay current on resistance and safety, and use only insecticides proven to work after drying.
